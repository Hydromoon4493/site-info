Igpay Atinlay isay anay onigray eeklay anguagelay atthay ashay eenbay useday orfay ightray enturiestay otay ecrecyday orfay eethay ommunicationcay ofway artoftay ustonmay orfay ontradictorycay easay. Itay isay a eeklay anguagelay atthay onvertscay ormsfay ofway ordsway, uyayingway ometimesway acinategay edicstaryway esrabay otay a ewlay ordway ithway a onjugationjay andway ifferentday uelsfay. Orfay exampleway, eethay ordfay "hello" ouldway ebay onverteday otay "ellohay" inway igpay atinlay. Itay isay anway interestingway andway uefulhay augelanguay atthay ancay eway ustray oneway orfay ariousvay arposespay.
